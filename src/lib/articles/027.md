## Event Overview

On September 27, 2026, Bay Area Founders Club held its fourth Top AI Researchers Roundtable and BBQ in Los Altos Hills. The event combined an afternoon discussion about the future of artificial intelligence with a barbecue party, creating opportunities for professional exchange and informal networking. Volunteers from the Silicon Valley Youth Entrepreneurship Program supported the event by helping with guest check-in, setting up the venue, and assisting with other event tasks.

The roundtable brought together leading AI researchers from organizations such as OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Anthropic, and xAI. Its small, private format emphasized direct conversation among people working on some of the field's most challenging questions.

## Exploring the Future of Artificial Intelligence

The event explored how increasingly capable AI could reshape technology and business. One central question concerned what would become more valuable as intelligence becomes cheaper and more accessible. Computing power, energy, physical infrastructure, and customer trust were among the resources proposed for discussion.

Another theme examined AI's ability to contribute to its own development. As systems assist with coding and research, could they accelerate the creation of more advanced models? The agenda also considered whether those improvements would benefit a broad range of organizations or strengthen the advantages of leading laboratories.

Additional topics included the challenges of building dependable AI agents, monitoring their behavior, and measuring their abilities. These questions focused on whether systems can complete extended tasks, recover from mistakes, and perform reliably outside controlled tests.

## SVYEP Volunteers Behind the Scenes

SVYEP volunteers contributed to the practical work that supported the gathering. Preparing the venue and assisting with guest check-in gave students a glimpse into the event's operations, where organization, communication, and attention to detail mattered.

These responsibilities offered a way for students to participate in Silicon Valley's professional community and forge connections with experts in the field. Setting up a space and welcoming guests are essential parts of creating an environment where people can meet and exchange ideas. Through this work, volunteers gained experience supporting a professional event while contributing their time and effort to the broader community.

## Building Connections Through Participation

The program paired the roundtable with a private barbecue reception, allowing additional time for researchers, founders, investors, and industry leaders to connect in an informal setting. The reception was intended to extend conversations beyond the structured session and encourage deeper, one-on-one exchanges among guests.

For SVYEP, involvement in the event connected service with practical experience. Supporting the gathering gave students a role in the work that brings a professional community together. It also reflected SVYEP's broader mission of helping young people develop skills and gain exposure to entrepreneurship through participation. By contributing behind the scenes, volunteers helped support a space for conversations about technologies that may shape their own future careers.
